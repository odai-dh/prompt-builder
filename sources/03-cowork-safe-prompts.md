Notes (own words):
- Destructive verbs are ambiguous ("cut", "update", "clean up"). When the wrong reading can't be undone, spell out the intended action and what must be kept.
- State bounds: which files, which time range, what not to touch, draft vs send.
- Unattended / scheduled runs should draft for review until the task has proven reliable.
