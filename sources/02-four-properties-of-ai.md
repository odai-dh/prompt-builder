Notes (own words):
- Next-token prediction: output continues patterns; it is not a lookup.
- Knowledge: frozen at a cutoff; recent facts must be supplied in the prompt.
- Working memory: limited context window; context helps until it overloads.
- Steerability: the model follows the pattern you set, so there is always a gap between intent and result.
