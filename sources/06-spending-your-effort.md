Notes (own words):
- Effort = how much compute/independence Claude spends: more verification, edge-case testing and own judgement at higher levels.
- Levels in Claude Code: low, medium, high, xhigh, max; change with /effort, even mid-conversation.
- Higher effort mostly fixes missed edge cases, not a wrong approach.
- Biggest gains: security, hardware, ML, science. Small gains on rulebook-style operations work.
- Vague tasks: higher effort = more assumptions made for you. Detailed spec: levels converge.
- Author's loop: have Claude interview you on a spec -> build on low -> review -> verify on high.
- Rule of thumb: low = quick/in the loop; medium = regular work; high = verification, edge cases, bug fixes in existing code; max = fully autonomous hard problems.
